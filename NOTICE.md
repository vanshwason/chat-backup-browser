Main changes from the original, starting in October 2026:

- Renamed to Chat Backup Browser, with a new logo, icons and welcome screen.
- Phone access: a private server (`server/`) that serves your chat exports from
  your computer to your phone, plus a WhatsApp-style mobile interface.
- Speed with very large chats: windowed message list, batched media gallery.
- Desktop app reopens the chats from the last session without prompting.
- Per-person media, view-once, call and reply statistics.
